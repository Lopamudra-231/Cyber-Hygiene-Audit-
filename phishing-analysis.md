# Phishing Email Analysis

### Example 1: Fake Bank Mail
- Sender: support@hdfc-bankk.com (Fake - extra 'k')
- Subject: URGENT: Your KYC Will Expire Today
- Red Flag: Urgent tone, Fake domain, Link to fake site

### Example 2: Fake Job Mail
- Sender: hr@job-offer-free.com
- Subject: You Are Selected For Job
- Red Flag: Asking for money / OTP

### How To Identify Phishing?
1. Check sender email spelling
2. Hover on link before clicking
3. Bank never asks OTP on mail
4. Urgent / Fear = Phishing Trick
